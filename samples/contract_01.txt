MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on
March 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

  - Faro Norte Logistics, S.A., a company incorporated in the Republic of
    Panama, with offices at Edificio Pacifico, Piso 9, Punta Pacifica,
    Panama City (the "Client"); and
  - Quetzal Software Group LLC, a limited liability company incorporated
    in the State of Florida, USA (the "Contractor").

1. SERVICES
   The Contractor will design, develop and maintain the Client's fleet
   management platform, including monthly feature releases and security
   patches delivered within 72 hours of vulnerability disclosure.

2. COMPENSATION
   The Client will pay the Contractor USD 4,500.00 per month, invoiced on
   the first business day of each month and payable net 30.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
   This Agreement runs from the Effective Date until February 28, 2027.
   It does not renew automatically; renewal requires written agreement of
   both parties. Either party may terminate for material breach upon 30
   days' written notice if the breach remains uncured.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
   Each party will keep the other party's non-public information
   confidential for a period of three (3) years after termination.

5. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Panama.

Signed on the Effective Date by the authorized representatives of both
parties.
